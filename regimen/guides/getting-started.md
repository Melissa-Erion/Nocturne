When you open Regimen for the first time, a short guided setup builds your whole plan: your calorie and macro targets, a workout schedule on the days you can train, and a full day of meals portioned to the gram. It's six short screens. You can change every answer later in **Me › Settings**, and your history is never lost.

> Just looking around? Tap **Explore with sample data** on the first screen to see six weeks of workouts, weigh-ins and meals before you set up your own.

## 1. Pick your goal

Choose what you're training for: **Fat loss**, **Muscle gain**, **Recomposition**, **Strength**, **Maintenance** or **General fitness**. This shapes your suggested calories. You still choose your own plan; Regimen organises it.

![Step 1: choose what you're training for](/img/app/onboarding-1.webp)

## 2. Tell it about you

Enter your current weight, height and age, plus your goal weight if you have one. Pick your sex (used only for the energy estimate), how active you are outside training, and your training experience. These numbers stay private and are used to estimate how much you need to eat.

![Step 2: about you](/img/app/onboarding-2.webp)

## 3. Choose your training days

Tap the days you can actually train. Regimen places your workouts on those days in rotation order and avoids putting back-to-back sessions on the same muscles. Set how long a session usually takes, your preferred workout time, where you train and what equipment you have.

![Step 3: your training week](/img/app/onboarding-3.webp)

> Pick the days you can train on a normal week, not your best week. Three days you keep beats five days you skip.

## 4. Check your daily targets

Regimen suggests calories, protein, carbs and fat from your answers. Protein is set to 1 gram per pound of body weight. Keep the suggestion, or type your own numbers. Choose how many meals a day you eat (2 to 6), and optionally set different targets for rest days. Add any dietary preferences, allergies or foods you never want to see.

![Step 4: daily targets and meals per day](/img/app/onboarding-4.webp)

## 5. Set your check-ins and reminders

Pick a weekly, every-two-weeks or monthly check-in day, when you'd like a workout reminder, a time for your morning weigh-in reminder, and quiet hours when Regimen stays silent.

![Step 5: check-ins and reminders](/img/app/onboarding-5.webp)

## 6. Review and save

Look over your answers and tap **Save & build schedule**. Your schedule fills in from today, and your first day of meals is already planned and portioned.

![Step 6: review your plan](/img/app/onboarding-6.webp)

## What happens next

You land on **Today**: your workout for today, the food you have left and your next meal. A one-minute app tour shows you around (you can replay it any time from **Me › Settings**). Next, read [Find your way around](/guides/find-your-way-around/).
