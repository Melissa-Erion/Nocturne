The scale alone can be misleading: water, salt and a late dinner all move it. Regimen looks at the trend and a few other signals so you can see what's really happening.

## Weigh in

Tap **Weight** under **Quick log** on Today, ideally at the same time each morning. Regimen shows a **7-day average**, so one high reading won't swing your trend.

## The 2-week calorie check

Every two weeks (once you have at least 8 weigh-ins over 2 weeks), Today shows **Your 2-week calorie check**. Regimen compares your weight trend with the pace your goal calls for:

- **On track**: your calories stay the same. Tap **Got it**.
- **Off track**: it suggests a small change, between 50 and 200 calories a day. Protein stays the same; carbs and fat absorb the change. Tap to apply it, or **Not now** to check again next week.

It never suggests going below a safe minimum. If you haven't been following your meals closely, choose Not now; the trend only means something when the plan was followed.

![The 2-week calorie check on Today](/img/app/today-2.webp)

## Check-ins

**Progress › Check-ins** is a short weekly (or every-two-weeks or monthly) review: weight, body measurements, and how your energy, hunger, sleep, stress and recovery have been. You'll see what's changed since your first check-in.

![Progress › Check-ins](/img/app/checkins.webp)

## Progress photos

**Progress › Photos** keeps front, side and back photos private to your account. Compare two dates side by side or with a slider. Photos are never shared or used for anything except your comparisons.

## Records and analytics

**Progress › Records** lists your heaviest lifts, best reps and estimated one-rep maxes. **Progress › Analytics** shows your weight trend and weekly rate, training volume, strength over time and how closely you've followed your meals.

![Progress › Analytics: your weight trend](/img/app/analytics.webp)

> Aim to lose about 0.5% of your body weight a week for fat loss, or gain about 0.25% a week for muscle. Faster isn't better: it usually costs muscle or adds mostly fat.
