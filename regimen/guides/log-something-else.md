Real life doesn't follow a meal plan. When you eat something that wasn't planned, don't scrap the day: log it, and Regimen re-portions the rest of it for you.

## Log it

1. On **Today**, under Food left today, tap **Log something else**. (It's also in **Eat › Meals**.)
2. Choose how to enter it:
  - **Find a food**: search the food list, pick it and enter how many grams you had.
  - **Quick estimate**: type what it was and the calories. Protein, carbs and fat are optional; if you only know the calories, Regimen assumes a typical mix.
3. Tap **Log it**.

![Log something else, with a quick estimate for a latte](/img/app/log-extra.webp)

## What happens next

Regimen counts it as eaten and trims the meals you haven't eaten yet so the day still lands on your targets. You'll see a message like *"Latte logged (190 kcal). Your remaining meals were trimmed by 180 kcal to keep today on target."* Each trimmed meal shows **Trimmed from …** under its target.

Meals you've already eaten, and meals you've marked as prepped, are left exactly as they are.

## Changed your mind?

Logged items are listed under Food left today and in Eat › Meals. Tap the ✕ next to one to remove it, and your meals go back up.

> If what you ate was bigger than everything you had left, your remaining meals can't go below zero. That's fine: one day over doesn't undo a week of good days. Just carry on tomorrow.
