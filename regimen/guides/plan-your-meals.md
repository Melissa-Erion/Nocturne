Open **Eat › Meals**. You don't start from a blank page: Regimen has already planned today's meals and worked out the grams of every food so the day adds up to your calorie, protein, carb and fat targets. Your job is just to make it yours.

![Eat › Meals: today is already planned](/img/app/meals.webp)

## Check each meal

Tap a meal (Breakfast, Lunch and so on) to open it. Each food shows the exact amount in grams and whether that's the **raw**, **cooked** or **prepared** weight, so you know when to weigh it. Weigh the food as shown and you'll hit the meal's target.

![A meal with every food weighed to the gram](/img/app/meals-2.webp)

## Change what you like

- **Add a food**: type in the search box under the foods ("chicken", "oats", "greek yogurt"). Pick a result and the grams of every food in the meal refit by themselves.
- **Swap a food**: tap **Swap** next to it. Regimen suggests foods of the same kind (a protein for a protein, a carb for a carb) with the amount already worked out. Tap **Use** and the rest of the meal is refitted around it.
- **Remove a food**: tap the ✕. The other foods refit to cover the gap.
- **Set an exact amount**: type a number of grams yourself. That food is then **locked** (the lock icon turns on) and stays put while the others adjust around it.

![Swap a food: alternatives with the amount already worked out](/img/app/swap.webp)

> An empty meal shows one-tap buttons for common foods: add a protein, a carb, veg and a fat, and the grams sort themselves out.

## Mark it as eaten

After each meal, tap **Mark as eaten** (or **Ate it** on Today). The Today screen then shows what you have left for the day. Tapping it again undoes it.

## Training days and rest days

The **Training day / Rest day** switch under the date picks which targets the day uses. Rest days can have their own targets, set in **Me › Settings › Nutrition**.

## How the day is split between meals

Curious how your daily targets are divided? Open **How your … kcal is split** at the bottom of the page. You can split them evenly, put more carbs around your workout, add extra protein to certain meals, or enter your own split.

## Plan ahead

Use the arrows next to the date, or **Plan tomorrow** under the meals, to plan other days. Then use **Prep** to batch-cook and **Groceries** to shop. See [Meal prep and your grocery list](/guides/meal-prep-and-groceries/).

> Today's meals are free for everyone. Planning other days, swaps and saved meals are part of Regimen Pro, which you can try free for 7 days.
