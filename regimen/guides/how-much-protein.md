If you lift weights and want to build or keep muscle, protein is the one number worth getting right. Here's the short version, and how Regimen handles it.

## The short answer

For people who train, research points to roughly **1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight per day** as the amount where most people get most of the muscle-building benefit, with some people benefiting up to about **2.2 g/kg**. In pounds, that's about **0.7 to 1 gram per pound**.

Regimen sets protein at **1 gram per pound** by default. That's the top of the useful range: simple to remember, covers people who need more, and it's especially helpful when you're eating less to lose fat, because extra protein helps you keep muscle and stay full.

> If 1 g per lb feels like a lot of food, anywhere from 0.7 g per lb up still gets most of the benefit. You can change your protein target any time in **Me › Settings › Nutrition**.

## Spread it out

Your body uses protein best when it's spread over the day rather than packed into dinner. Aim for roughly **25 to 40 grams per meal** across 3 to 5 meals. Regimen does this for you: it splits your daily target across your meals and portions each one.

## Easy ways to get 30 g

- 125 g of cooked chicken breast
- 1 can of tuna (about 140 g drained) with a little left over
- 300 g of plain Greek yogurt (0%)
- 250 g of cottage cheese
- 4 whole eggs plus 100 g of egg whites
- 200 g of firm tofu plus a glass of soy milk

## Does more protein hurt?

For healthy adults, protein intakes in this range are considered safe. If you have kidney disease or another medical condition, talk to your doctor before raising your protein.

> Regimen's numbers are planning estimates for healthy adults, not medical advice.
