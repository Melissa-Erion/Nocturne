Every workout in Regimen tells you exactly what to lift. You just do the sets and tap a button.

## Start it

On **Today**, tap **Start workout** (or start any planned workout from **Train › Schedule**). The workout opens full screen.

## One exercise at a time

The row at the top lists the exercises in today's workout. The current one is highlighted, and finished ones get a tick. Tap any of them to jump to it.

Under the exercise name you'll see **Target today**: the weight and reps to aim for, worked out from what you lifted last time. Below it are your last session and your best.

![A workout: today's target and the sets](/img/app/workout.webp)

> First time doing an exercise? There's no history yet, so the target says to pick a weight you could lift for a couple more reps. That session becomes your starting point.

## Log each set

Each set has boxes for the weight, reps and **RIR** (reps in reserve: how many more you could have done). They're filled in from your target.

- If you did what it says, just tap the big **Log set** button.
- If you did something different, change the weight or reps first, then tap **Log set**.
- Sets marked **W** are warm-ups. Tap a set's number to switch it between warm-up and working set.

Logging a set starts the **rest timer** at the top. Use **−15**, **+15** or **Skip rest** as you like. When the last set of an exercise is logged, Regimen moves on to the next exercise.

![The rest timer counting down between sets](/img/app/workout-rest.webp)

## Handy extras

- **Swap** (the arrows icon): pick a different exercise for today, or replace it in your plan.
- **Info**: how to do the exercise.
- **Notes & how it felt**: jot down a cue or flag discomfort.
- **Pause**: stops both timers if you're interrupted.
- The arrow at the top **minimises** the workout so you can check something else. A banner brings you back.

## Finish

When every set is done, the big button becomes **Finish & save**. You'll see your working sets, total volume and any personal records, and your next session's targets update straight away. Only completed sets are saved; if you stop early, you can still save what you did.
