Skipping the warm-up because it takes too long? This one takes five minutes, and your first working sets will feel smoother for it.

## The routine

1. **1 minute of easy cardio**: bike, rower or a brisk walk. Just get warm.
2. **10 bodyweight squats**: slow on the way down, full range.
3. **10 glute bridges**: squeeze for a second at the top.
4. **15 band pull-aparts**: no band? Do arm circles both ways.
5. **2 ramp-up sets** of your first exercise: about 50% of your working weight, then about 75%, for a few easy reps each.

## Why ramp-up sets matter

The general warm-up raises your body temperature. The ramp-up sets rehearse the exact movement you're about to do with lighter weights, so your first real set isn't also your practice set.

> In Regimen, warm-up sets are marked **W** in your workout and don't count toward your working sets or progression. Tap a set's number to turn it into a warm-up.
