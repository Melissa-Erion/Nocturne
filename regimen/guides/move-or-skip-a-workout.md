Your schedule lives in **Train › Schedule**. Workouts are placed on your training days in rotation order (for example Lower 1, Upper 1, Lower 2, Upper 2), and the rotation always continues where it left off.

![Train › Schedule: your week of workouts](/img/app/schedule.webp)

## Move a workout

- **From Today**: tap **Move to another day** under today's workout and pick a date.
- **From the schedule**: tap a workout and choose a new day, or drag it onto another day.

**Shift later workouts** decides what happens if the new day already has a workout. When it's on, that workout moves to the next training day (and so on, only as far as needed). When it's off, both workouts stay on that day.

## Skip a workout

Tap **Skip** under today's workout, or skip it from the schedule. The rotation simply continues with the next workout on your next training day; nothing is lost.

## Pause for a trip or a sick week

Tap **Pause schedule**, pick a reason (illness, travel, injury, recovery or other) and a start date. Add an end date if you know it. Planned workouts in the pause come off your calendar. When you **Resume**, your rotation picks up with the next workout in order.

## Undo and reset

Made a mistake? **Undo** reverses your last change. **Reset schedule** rebuilds your future schedule from your plan and training days (your logged workouts are kept).

## Sync to your calendar

Tap **Sync to calendar** to get a private link for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Your workouts appear with their exercise list and stay in sync when you move or skip one. Calendar apps refresh on their own schedule, so changes can take a few hours to show.

> Change your training days, workout times (even a different time for each day) or session length any time in **Me › Settings › Training**.
