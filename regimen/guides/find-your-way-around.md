Everything in Regimen lives in four tabs. On a phone they're at the bottom of the screen; on a computer they're in the menu on the left. Each tab has a row of sections at the top of the page.

## Today: what do I do now?

Your day at a glance. Today's workout (with a big **Start workout** button and the target for each exercise), the food you have left today, your next meal, and **Quick log** buttons for your weight, water and a progress photo.

![Today: your workout, food left today and quick log](/img/app/today.webp)

## Train: what's my week?

- **Schedule**: your workouts on your training days. Move, skip or pause them.
- **Plans**: the workouts in your rotation and their exercises. Add your own exercises if one isn't listed.
- **History**: every set you've logged.

When you start a workout it opens full screen. The arrow at the top minimises it, and a banner brings you back.

## Eat: what do I eat?

- **Meals**: today's meals, already planned and portioned. See [Plan your meals to the gram](/guides/plan-your-meals/).
- **Macros**: today's targets, what's left, and water.
- **Prep**: batch-cook amounts for the week.
- **Recipes**: your recipes and saved meals.
- **Groceries**: a shopping list built from your prep.

## Progress: is it working?

**Check-ins**, private **Photos**, personal **Records** and **Analytics** (weight trend, training volume and more).

## Me: settings and account

Tap the round person icon at the top right (on a computer, **Me** at the bottom of the menu). **Settings** has your goal, targets, training days, reminders and the **Appearance** switch for light or dark mode. **Account** has your email, password and subscription.

![Me › Settings, with the Appearance switch](/img/app/settings.webp)

> Tabs remember where you were. If you left Train on History, tapping Train takes you back to History.
