The hardest part of the gym is the first week: not knowing what to do, which machines to use, or how heavy to go. This plan keeps it simple so you can focus on showing up.

## The plan

Three days a week, alternating two full-body workouts. About 45 minutes each.

### Day A

- Goblet squat: 3 sets of 10
- Dumbbell bench press: 3 sets of 10
- Seated cable row: 3 sets of 12
- Plank: 3 × 30 seconds

### Day B

- Dumbbell Romanian deadlift: 3 sets of 10
- Lat pulldown: 3 sets of 10
- Glute bridge: 3 sets of 12
- Dead bug: 3 × 8 per side

Rest about 90 seconds between sets.

## The week

- Monday: Day A
- Wednesday: Day B
- Friday: Day A
- Next week, start with Day B and keep alternating.

## How heavy?

Pick weights that feel **easy** this week. Stop each set when you could still do about two more reps. You're learning the movements and finding your starting weights, not testing yourself.

> Write down what you lifted. Next time, try one more rep, or a little more weight when every set feels easy. That's how you get stronger.

## Before each workout

Do the [5-minute warm-up](/guides/the-5-minute-warm-up/). It makes your first working sets feel smoother.
