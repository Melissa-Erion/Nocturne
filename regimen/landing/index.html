Features

Everything between “I want to get in shape” and knowing exactly what to do today.

A workout schedule that builds itself

Choose your training days and Regimen places every workout in order. Life happens: move, skip or pause a session and your rotation stays on track.

  • Ready-made upper/lower plans for 4 or 5 days, or build your own
  • Move a workout and later ones shift only as far as needed
  • Undo any change, or reset the schedule from your plan
  • Live sync to Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar

Know exactly what to lift next time

Log your sets with a built-in rest timer. Regimen uses progressive overload to give you a specific weight and rep target for every exercise in your next session.

  • Double or linear progression, using the weight jumps your gym actually has
  • Swap an exercise mid-workout, or pause and pick up later
  • Strength, volume and body-weight trends that ignore daily noise
  • Weekly check-ins and private progress photos

Calendar sync

Your workouts appear in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar and update when you move or skip one.

Phone, tablet or computer

Works in any browser and syncs across your devices. A guided tour shows you around on day one.

Private by design

Progress photos stay in private storage only you can see. No ads, and your data is never sold.

Meal planning

The meal planner that does the math, down to the gram.

Most apps make you log food after you eat it. Regimen plans it before you do: it knows your targets for today, splits them across your meals and works out exactly how much of each food to eat.

Exact portions for every meal

Tell Regimen how many meals you eat and when. It splits your calories, protein, carbs and fat across them, then solves the grams of each food you choose so every meal lands on target. If a meal can't match perfectly, it shows you the difference instead of hiding it.

  • Protein set automatically to 1 gram per pound of body weight, or enter your own custom macros
  • Separate targets and meal times for training days and rest days, applied automatically from your schedule
  • Lock a food at the amount you want and Regimen rebalances the rest of the meal
  • Cooked or raw weights, labelled on every food, so the numbers match your kitchen scale

Meal prep without the spreadsheet

Choose a recipe or meal and how many containers you need. Regimen tells you how much raw food to buy and cook, using typical cooking yields. Weigh the cooked batch and it gives you the exact amount for each container.

  • Raw amounts to buy and weigh, plus the expected cooked weight
  • Portions per container for training-day or rest-day targets
  • Cooking for two? Set the number of people
  • A grocery list built from your prep, grouped by aisle, with check-offs

Smart macro split

Spread macros evenly, put more carbs before and after your workout, add extra protein to the meals you choose, or set your own split.

Food swaps that match

Out of chicken? Swap any food for one that matches its protein, carbs or fat, with the new amount calculated and every difference shown. Your food preferences, allergies and exclusions are respected.

Recipes and favourite meals

Build recipes by weight, enter the cooked weight and get nutrition per serving. Save favourite meals and load them in one tap.

Any food, from any source

Search USDA FoodData Central, scan a barcode, add a food from its nutrition label, or save a restaurant meal as a clearly labelled estimate.

Planned vs logged

Tap to log a meal as you eat it. Your nutrition dashboard shows what's left for the day, planned versus logged, and your water.

Your meals, your way

From 2 to 6 meals a day, named and timed the way you eat. Vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free or gluten-free: you choose what goes in, Regimen handles the numbers.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Regimen?

Regimen is an all-in-one fitness planning app. It schedules your workouts, calculates your calorie and macro targets, plans your meals with exact gram portions, and tells you exactly what weight and reps to aim for in every session.

How does Regimen calculate my calories and macros?

Regimen estimates your daily energy needs with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and your activity level, then adjusts for your goal. Protein is set automatically to 1 gram per pound of body weight, and fat and carbs fill the rest. Prefer your own numbers? Enter custom macros at any time, and set different targets for training days and rest days.

Is Regimen free?

Yes, you can start free. The free version includes the guided setup, your calculated calorie and macro targets, a preview of your training week and weight logging. Regimen Pro unlocks everything else and costs CA$179.99 per year or CA$24.99 per month, with a 7-day free trial.

Does Regimen tell me what weight to lift?

Yes. Regimen uses progressive overload (double progression or linear progression) to give an exact weight and rep target for every exercise in your next session, based on what you logged last time.

Can Regimen plan meals with exact portions?

Yes. Pick the foods you like and Regimen calculates the grams of each food so every meal matches its share of your daily protein, carbs and fat. You can lock a food at a set amount, swap foods for macro-matched alternatives, and get meal-prep amounts per container plus a grocery list.

Does Regimen sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. Regimen gives you a private calendar link for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Your workouts, including the exercise list, stay in sync when you move or skip a session.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Regimen works today in any web browser on your phone, tablet or computer, and your data syncs across devices. Native iPhone and Android apps are coming soon.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel before the 7-day trial ends and you are not charged. If you cancel later, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your data is kept.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored securely in your private Regimen account and syncs across your devices. Progress photos are kept in private storage that only you can access, and Regimen does not sell your data or show ads. See the privacy policy.

Is Regimen medical advice?

No. Regimen's numbers are planning estimates for healthy adults. Check with a qualified professional if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or plan large changes to your diet or training.
