    Questions

    Before you start.

    What is Regimen?

    Regimen is an all-in-one fitness planning app. It schedules your workouts, calculates your calorie and macro targets, plans your meals with exact gram portions, and tells you exactly what weight and reps to aim for in every session.

    How does Regimen calculate my calories and macros?

    Regimen estimates your daily energy needs with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and your activity level, then adjusts for your goal. Protein is set automatically to 1 gram per pound of body weight, and fat and carbs fill the rest. Prefer your own numbers? Enter custom macros at any time, and set different targets for training days and rest days.

    Is Regimen free?

    Yes, you can start free. The free version includes the guided setup, your calculated calorie and macro targets, a preview of your training week and weight logging. Regimen Pro unlocks everything else and costs CA$179.99 per year or CA$24.99 per month, with a 7-day free trial.

    Does Regimen tell me what weight to lift?

    Yes. Regimen uses progressive overload (double progression or linear progression) to give an exact weight and rep target for every exercise in your next session, based on what you logged last time.

    Can Regimen plan meals with exact portions?

    Yes. Pick the foods you like and Regimen calculates the grams of each food so every meal matches its share of your daily protein, carbs and fat. You can lock a food at a set amount, swap foods for macro-matched alternatives, and get meal-prep amounts per container plus a grocery list.

    Does Regimen sync with Google Calendar?

    Yes. Regimen gives you a private calendar link for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Your workouts, including the exercise list, stay in sync when you move or skip a session.

    Is there an iPhone or Android app?

    Regimen works today in any web browser on your phone, tablet or computer, and your data syncs across devices. Native iPhone and Android apps are coming soon.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel before the 7-day trial ends and you are not charged. If you cancel later, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your data is kept.

    Where is my data stored?

    Your data is stored securely in your private Regimen account and syncs across your devices. Progress photos are kept in private storage that only you can access, and Regimen does not sell your data or show ads. See the privacy policy.

    Is Regimen medical advice?

    No. Regimen's numbers are planning estimates for healthy adults. Check with a qualified professional if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or plan large changes to your diet or training.
