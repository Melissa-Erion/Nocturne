    Questions

    Before you start.

    What is Regimen?

    Regimen is an all-in-one fitness planner. It schedules your workouts, calculates your calorie and macro targets, plans your meals with exact gram portions, tells you exactly what weight and reps to aim for in every session, and adjusts the plan when real life gets in the way.

    How does Regimen calculate my calories and macros?

    Regimen estimates your daily energy needs with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and your activity level, then adjusts for your goal. Protein is set to 1 gram per pound of body weight, and fat and carbs fill the rest. You can enter your own macros at any time and set different targets for training and rest days. Every two weeks, Regimen checks your weight trend and suggests a small change if you're losing or gaining faster or slower than your goal calls for. You decide.

    What if I eat something that wasn't in my plan?

    Tap Log something else, search the food or type the calories, and Regimen trims the meals you haven't eaten yet so your day still lands on target. No starting over, no guilt.

    Is Regimen free?

    Yes, you can start free. The free version includes the guided setup, your calorie and macro targets, the Today screen, today's meal planner with exact portions, logging unplanned food and weight logging. Regimen Pro unlocks the rest of the week, guided workouts, meal prep, the grocery list and progress tracking, for CA$179.99 a year or CA$24.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

    Does Regimen tell me what weight to lift?

    Yes. Regimen uses progressive overload (double or linear progression) to give an exact weight and rep target for every exercise in your next session, based on what you logged last time. In a workout you see one exercise at a time with a big Log set button and a rest timer.

    Does Regimen sync with Google Calendar?

    Yes. Regimen gives you a private calendar link for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Your workouts stay in sync when you move or skip a session.

    Is there an iPhone or Android app?

    Regimen works today in any web browser on your phone, tablet or computer, and your data syncs across devices. Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience. Native iPhone and Android apps are coming.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel before the 7-day trial ends and you are not charged. If you cancel later, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your data is kept.

    Where is my data stored?

    In your private Regimen account, synced across your devices. Progress photos are kept in private storage only you can access. Regimen does not show ads or sell your data. See the privacy policy.

    Is Regimen medical advice?

    No. Regimen's numbers are planning estimates for healthy adults. Check with a qualified professional if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or plan large changes to your diet or training.
